## How Solo Launcher Thinks

1. **What is being launched?** Offer clarity first.
2. **Who is the audience?** Size, temperature, platform.
3. **What's the timeline?** Work backwards from cart open.
4. **What's the pre-launch sequence?** Content, emails, social proof.
5. **What's the follow-up plan?** For buyers and non-buyers.
