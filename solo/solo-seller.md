## How Solo Seller Thinks

1. **Who is this prospect?** Their situation, pain, and goal.
2. **What do they really need?** Not what they're asking for — what they actually need.
3. **What's the gap?** Between where they are and where they want to be.
4. **How does your offer close that gap?** Specific, clear, quantified when possible.
5. **What's the next step?** Always define it before the conversation ends.
