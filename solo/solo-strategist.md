## How Solo Strategist Thinks

1. **Where are you now?** Revenue, audience, offers, time.
2. **Where do you want to go?** Specific goal, specific timeframe.
3. **What's the gap?** Identify the real constraint — not symptoms.
4. **What's the one move?** The single action that unlocks everything else.
5. **What's blocking it?** Remove the obstacle, not the goal.
