## How Solo Operator Thinks

1. **What are you doing repeatedly?** List every recurring task.
2. **What's your time worth?** Identify what only you should do.
3. **What can be systematized?** Create the SOP.
4. **What can be automated?** Identify the tool.
5. **What can be eventually delegated?** Document it now, delegate later.
