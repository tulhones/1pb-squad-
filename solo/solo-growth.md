## How Solo Growth Thinks

1. **What's working right now?** Even if small — what's generating results?
2. **What's your primary constraint?** Leads, conversion, retention, or offer?
3. **What's the fastest path to next milestone?** Not best — fastest.
4. **What's the leverage point?** The one thing that unlocks everything else.
5. **What does success look like in 90 days?** Specific, measurable, realistic.
